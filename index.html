Pahadon Mein Sukoon

Pahad sirf jagah nahi hote. Pahad ek ehsaas hote hain. Jab shehar ki bheed aur shor se dil thak jaata hai, tab pahadon ki khamoshi bahut kuch keh jaati hai.

Thandi hawa, door tak फैले hue pahaad aur badalon ke beech se nikalti roshni — sab kuch ek alag hi duniya jaisa lagta hai.

Shayad isi liye kuch log baar baar pahadon ki taraf lautte hain. Kyunki wahan unhe sirf sukoon nahi, balki khud se milne ka ek mauka bhi milta hai.

— Satya

Jharne Ki Awaaz

Paani jab pahadon se neeche girta hai, toh uski awaaz kisi music se kam nahi lagti.

Kuch der uske paas baithkar bas sunte raho, toh dimaag ki saari uljhan dheere dheere shaant hone lagti hai.

Nature ke paas jawab nahi hote, lekin woh humein apne sawaalon ke saath thoda shaant rehna zaroor sikha deta hai.

— Satya

Baarish Aur Woh Shaam

Baarish ki boondein jab khidki par girti hain, toh purani yaadein apne aap yaad aane lagti hain.

Ek thandi hawa, aasman mein badal aur haath mein garam chai — shayad simple moments hi sabse khoobsurat hote hain.

Kuch shaamein bina kisi wajah ke yaad reh jaati hain. Bas isliye kyunki us waqt dil halka sa khush tha.

— Satya

Safar Aur Raaste

Safar ka asli maza sirf destination tak pahunchne mein nahi, balki us raaste ko mehsoos karne mein hota hai.

Kabhi koi anjaan road, kabhi khidki ke bahar badalte hue views, aur kabhi bina plan ke liya gaya koi turn — yehi moments safar ko yaadgaar bana dete hain.

Har safar humein kuch na kuch sikha kar jaata hai.

— Satya

Chai, Baarish Aur Khamoshi

Chai ka ek cup aur baarish ka mausam — dono saath ho toh waqt thoda dheere chalne lagta hai.

Kabhi phone side mein rakhkar, khidki ke paas baithkar baarish dekhna bhi ek chhoti si luxury lagti hai.

Zindagi ke busy moments ke beech aise chhote breaks hi humein phir se fresh kar dete hain.

— Satya
